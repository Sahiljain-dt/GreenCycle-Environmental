Size Reduction & Material Processing

GreenCycle Environmental processes recoverable and end-of-life materials through a combination of sorting, size reduction, consolidation, and plastics processing. Our equipment and hands-on processing capabilities help prepare materials for recycling, reuse, downstream manufacturing, or destruction and appropriate disposal when continued use is no longer practical.

  • Baling: Compacting recyclable materials into dense, manageable bales for efficient storage, transportation, and downstream recycling.
  • Shredding: Reducing paper, plastics, and other materials into smaller, more manageable sizes for further processing, recycling, or destruction.
  • Grinding: Processing rigid, bulky, and oversized plastics into more uniform material suitable for recycling and further processing.
  • Extrusion: Blending and compounding recovered plastics to produce a homogenous reprocessed material for reuse and manufacturing applications.
  • Heavy Hand Sort Segregation: Manually separating mixed material streams to recover valuable commodities, remove contaminants, and improve the quality of recyclable materials.
  • Material Consolidation: Combining compatible recyclable materials and smaller loads into efficient shipments for transportation, storage, and downstream processing.
